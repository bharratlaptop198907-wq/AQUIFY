Considered materials

Simple packaging. Smarter choices.

We treat every component as a design decision — from bottle walls to labels and transport packs.

Bottle materials

Designed around practical recyclability, product safety and the durability needed for real-world handling.

Labels & inks

Clear information, restrained printing and a visual system that keeps the bottle feeling clean rather than crowded.

Secondary packs

Transport packaging is considered for strength, efficient stacking and opportunities to reduce unnecessary material.

AQUIFY material philosophy

Use less. Use thoughtfully. Recover what you can.

Our goal is not to make packaging disappear from the conversation. It is to make the conversation more intentional — by weighing protection, functionality, recyclability and the realities of local waste systems.
